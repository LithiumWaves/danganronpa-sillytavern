How mood music works
MOODS lists are used only when Dynamic Songs is on, during daytime, nighttime and investigations. The sprites on screen vote for a mood: Happy (joy, excitement), Sad (sadness, grief), Dramatic (realization, surprise), Tense (fear, nervousness), Angry (anger, disgust), Romantic (love, embarrassment). Neutral sprites don't vote. SCAN on the music player re-reads the sprites and changes the track; its list button browses songs by mood or phase.

Single-chat overworld
Explore without leaving your group chat: people in the room are unmuted, everyone else is muted. Install the Presence extension so absent characters also forget those lines.
Sprite click gifts
Click a character's sprite while exploring to give them a gift.
Cards' greetings and scenarios get pulled into new chats. Keep them, or strip them from that chat only (cards aren't edited).
When Monokuma hands out a motive by himself, counting from the day a chapter starts. A motive secretly sets up the chapter's murder. You can always start one from the phase readout.
How often Monokuma's murder plan isn't a plain "one killer, one victim": the wrong victim, an attacker who dies, two bodies, a suicide, an accomplice, or someone who only thinks they did it. Never the same twist twice in a row.
Mixed: besides announcements, Monokuma sometimes hands everyone a personal item with hidden contents (DVDs, secrets, motive videos; yours goes in Key Items), or afflicts a few students with a condition until the case closes. Every motive has a penalty if nobody kills by the deadline, and a murder can be foiled by staying with the killer or the victim.
Some mornings, a classmate proposes a get-together (a party, a study session, a séance) through one hidden AI call. Classmates can always propose one in the roleplay, and you can plan one with PLAN EVENT. When its time comes, everyone invited gathers in its room.
Examine during daily life
The overworld's EXAMINE works outside investigations too: look around any room for what you notice, Monocoins tucked into a few spots, and one hidden Monokuma per floor. Fresh each chapter. Never evidence.
Body discovery needs 3 witnesses
Like the games: the body discovery announcement (and the investigation) only starts once three people besides the killer have seen the body. Walking in alone plays the shock; bring others or wait for them. Who saw it, in order, becomes evidence. Off: the announcement plays as soon as you find it.
Show key evidence found
During an investigation the phase readout shows "EVIDENCE 2/6": how many of the clues Monokuma's plan hid have turned up (by examining, or a witness telling you). Evidence that turns up in the roleplay on its own isn't counted. A small hint at how much is left.
Confirm grave punishments
Some School Regulations are grave (mark them in RULES): breaking one is punished by death, like Mukuro in the first game, with a pink blood splash and the student marked dead. On: you're asked first, and saying no gives a final warning instead. You're never punished yourself, only warned.
When you examine something with classmates you're investigating with ("Investigate With" in the explore menu), one of them reacts in the chat.
A normal chat message, or a line shown only in the VN box (like what you notice when examining), generated the same way but never added to the chat.
Trial minigames use the case
Hangman's Gambit, Question Time, Question Truth, Mind Mine and Argument Armament are built from what really happened, and the vote's right answer is the real killer. Debates (Non-Stop, Mass Panic, Scrum, Rebuttal) never are, so you can still steer them wrong.
